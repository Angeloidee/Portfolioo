About

I am a telecommunications engineering student specialising in RF, microwave systems and signal processing, currently pursuing my Master's degree at the Universidad de Alicante.

My RF work focuses on the design and full-wave EM validation of passive microwave components — combining analytical transmission-line models with Ansys HFSS simulation to bridge the gap between theory and physical EM behaviour.

Alongside RF design, my Master's thesis investigates the application of machine learning to early Parkinson's disease detection through voice analysis — extracting acoustic biomarkers from sustained phonation and running speech to build classification models capable of identifying early-stage neurological changes.

This portfolio documents both tracks of my engineering work as they develop.
